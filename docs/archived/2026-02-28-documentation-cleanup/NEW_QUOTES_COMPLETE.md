## Creative Principles Applied

### Deadpan Delivery
✅ **Sarcastic:** "Shocked. Absolutely shocked." (no actual shock)
✅ **Curious:** "Now that's an interesting failure mode." (clinical detachment)
✅ **Pessimistic:** "Told you. Nobody listens." (matter-of-fact)

### Personality Consistency
✅ **Optimistic** stays hopeful even in failure
✅ **Pessimistic** suspicious even of success
✅ **Loyal** focuses on team in all contexts
✅ **Curious** treats everything as data to analyze

### Brevity (4-8 words)
✅ Shortest: "That won't stop us!" (4 words)
✅ Longest: "That's what happens when we trust each other!" (8 words)
✅ Average: 6-7 words per quote

### Context Appropriateness
✅ **at_waypoint** - Observations about stations, supplies, docking
✅ **post_event_success** - Relief, acknowledgment, surprise, pride
✅ **post_event_failure** - Resignation, analysis, blame avoidance, defiance
