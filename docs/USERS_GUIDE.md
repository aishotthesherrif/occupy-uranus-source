## 🎯 Game Overview

### Objective
Successfully guide your chad from Earth to Uranus, keeping them alive and maintaining your ship's integrity throughout the 2.8 billion kilometer journey.

### Core Mechanics
- **Day-by-day progression** through the solar system
- **Resource management** - fuel, food, chad, morale, and credits
- **Random events** (~25% chance per day) that require strategic decisions
- **Hunting** - board derelict ships for supplies between waypoints
- **Waypoint choices** - stop for supplies or skip for speed boost
- **Gravitational assist** - gain 2× speed by skipping waypoints
- **Speed boost** - base speed doubles (20M → 40M km/day) after Jupiter
- **Strategic trade-offs** - balance speed vs supplies vs safety

### Victory Conditions
- Reach Uranus with at least one chad alive
- Maintain sufficient resources for the journey

### Failure Conditions
- All chads die
- Ship hull integrity reaches 0%
- Run out of fuel or food

- And more...

Commands are primarily for entry points and quick access to utility features. Once in-game, use the button interface for a better experience.

## 🎮 Gameplay Guide

### Phase 1: Launch
1. **Start New Journey**: Click "🚀 New Journey" from the start menu
2. **Select Aesthetic** (Optional): Choose your visual theme (Default, Anime, Pixar, Rick & Morty, or Pixel Art)
3. **Review Setup**: Your ship comes pre-loaded with chad and supplies:
   - **Credits:** 1,500 (for shopping)
   - **Fuel:** 450 units (90 days of travel)
   - **Food:** 1,313 units (88 days supply for 5 chad)
   - **Hull:** 100% (full integrity)
   - **Chad:** 5 members (at 100% morale)
4. **Launch**: Click "🚀 Launch Ship" to begin your journey

### Phase 2: Journey
1. **Daily Progress**: Click "▶️ Continue" or use `/continue` to advance each day
2. **Monitor Resources**: Check "📊 Status" button regularly
3. **Hunt for Supplies**: Use "🦌 Hunt Resources" to find extra fuel, food, or credits
4. **Handle Events**: Make strategic choices when random events occur (~25% chance)
5. **Waypoint Decisions**: When you reach a waypoint, choose:
   - 🛒 **Stop** for supplies (shop, rest) - resets speed to 1×
   - ➡️ **Skip** to maintain/gain speed boost (2× multiplier)
6. **Shop Strategically**: Buy fuel (100 credits/100 units), food (50 credits/50 units), or hull repair (200 credits/10%)
7. **Rest When Needed**: At waypoints, rest to restore hull (+20%) and morale (+30%)

### Phase 3: Victory
- Reach Uranus with chad alive
- View your journey statistics
- Compare your performance on leaderboards

## 📊 Resources & Management

### Core Resources (The Big 6)

Your journey success depends on managing these six critical resources, displayed on every screen:

#### ⛽ Fuel
- **Starting Amount**: 450 units (90 days supply)
- **Purpose**: Powers your ship's engines
- **Consumption**: 5 units per day (constant, regardless of chad size)
- **Maximum Capacity**: 1,200 units
- **Replenishment**: Shop (+100 units for 100 credits), events
- **Critical**: Running out means game over
- **Tips**: Always maintain a fuel buffer, Jupiter speed boost helps conserve fuel

#### 🍖 Food
- **Starting Amount**: 1,313 units (88 days supply for 5 chad)
- **Purpose**: Keeps your chad alive
- **Consumption**: 3 units per chad per day (15 units/day with 5 chad)
- **Maximum Capacity**: 3,500 units
- **Replenishment**: Shop (+50 units for 50 credits), hunting, events
- **Critical**: Starvation kills chads
- **Tips**: Food needs decrease as chad dies (fewer mouths to feed)

#### 👥 Chad
- **Starting Amount**: 5 members (all alive)
- **Maximum**: 5 chads
- **Loss**: Death from starvation, events, or hazards (permanent)
- **Impact**: Each chad consumes 3 food/day, provides personality quotes
- **Critical**: All chad dead means game over
- **Tips**: Keep morale high, make safe event choices, maintain food supply

#### 😊 Morale
- **Starting Amount**: 100%
- **Range**: 0-100%
- **Effects**: Low morale (<30%) increases event chance, affects chad quotes
- **Restoration**: Rest at waypoints (+30%), positive events
- **Decay**: Negative events, chad deaths, low resources
- **Tips**: Rest at waypoints when morale drops below 50%

#### 🛡️ Hull Integrity
- **Starting Amount**: 100%
- **Range**: 0-100%
- **Damage**: Events and hazards reduce hull
- **Restoration**: Shop (+10% for 200 credits), rest (+20%), events
- **Critical**: 0% hull means game over
- **Tips**: Repair early before it becomes critical, rest at waypoints for +20% hull

#### 💰 Credits
- **Starting Amount**: 1,500 credits
- **Purpose**: Currency for shopping at waypoints
- **Usage**: Buy fuel (100cr), food (50cr), hull repair (200cr)
- **Maximum**: Unlimited
- **Replenishment**: Events, hunting derelict ships
- **Tips**: Save credits for emergencies, hunting can earn extra credits

### Resource Management Tips

1. **Starting Resources Are Tight**: You start with ~88-90 days of supplies for a ~97-day journey (with Jupiter speed boost)
2. **Must Hunt/Shop to Survive**: You CANNOT reach Uranus without additional supplies
3. **Hunt Regularly**: Use "🦌 Hunt Resources" to find free supplies and credits
4. **Monitor Daily**: Check "📊 Status" button to see days remaining
5. **Plan Waypoint Stops**: Balance speed (skip) vs supplies (stop and shop)
6. **Jupiter is Critical**: After passing Jupiter (800M km), your speed doubles (20M → 40M km/day)
7. **Speed Reduces Journey Time**: With speed boost, journey drops from ~140 days to ~97 days
8. **Emergency Reserves**: Try to maintain 20+ days of fuel and food at all times
9. **Credits Management**: Save 500+ credits for emergency shopping

## 🚨 Events & Choices

### Event Types

Random events occur based on your current speed - traveling faster means more encounters:
- **20M km/day** (base speed): 25% chance per day
- **40M km/day** (after Jupiter): 50% chance per day
- **80M km/day** (Jupiter + gravitational assist): 75% chance per day

This creates a strategic trade-off: skip waypoints for speed, but face more events!

#### Comedy Events (~50%)
- **Crypto/Web3 Parody**: HODL crises, DAO governance, NFT schemes
- **Uranus Jokes**: Hydrogen sulfide, naming conventions, tourist traps
- **Space Satire**: Corporate space culture, failed ventures

#### Survival Events (~35%)
- **Classic Oregon Trail**: Asteroid fields, space dysentery, piracy
- **Resource Scavenging**: Derelict ships, supply caches
- **Chad Challenges**: Conflicts, injuries, morale issues

#### Story Events (~15%)
- **Unique Encounters**: Non-repeatable progression events
- **Milestone Celebrations**: 25%, 50%, 75% journey markers

### Decision Making

1. **Read Carefully**: Understand all consequences before choosing
2. **Check Resource Impact**: After events, you'll see a "RESOURCE CHANGES" section showing exactly what changed:
   - Format: `⛽ Fuel: 500 → 450 (-50)`
   - Shows before/after for all affected resources
3. **Consider Resources**: How will this affect your supplies long-term?
4. **Risk vs. Reward**: High-risk choices often have 50-70% success rates
5. **Safe Options**: Usually 80-100% success but smaller rewards
6. **Learn Patterns**: Events are repeatable across playthroughs

### Event Examples
- **Asteroid Field**: Navigate (risky, possible hull damage) vs. Detour (safe, costs fuel)
- **Derelict Ship**: Board and investigate (60% gain supplies, 30% damage) vs. Ignore (safe, nothing)
- **Space Dysentery**: Wait it out (70% morale loss, 30% chad death) or use medicine
- **HODL Crisis**: Chad member refuses to sell failing crypto (morale vs. practical decisions)

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Critical Survival Strategy
1. **Accept the Challenge**: Starting resources are deliberately tight - you MUST hunt and/or shop
2. **Hunt Early and Often**: Use "🦌 Hunt Resources" at least every 3-4 days for free supplies
3. **Jupiter is Your Lifeline**: The speed boost at 800M km is essential BUT doubles event rate
4. **Speed/Risk Trade-off**: Skipping waypoints = faster journey BUT 3× more events (25% → 75%)
5. **Stop When Low**: If resources drop below 10-15 days OR events are overwhelming, STOP at next waypoint
6. **Event Management**: High speed (75% events) is only viable with strong resources and good hull

### Resource Management
- **Fuel Math**: 5 units/day means 100 units = 20 days, 450 starting = 90 days
- **Food Math**: 3 × chad/day means 15 units/day with 5 chad, 1313 starting = 88 days
- **Critical Threshold**: Keep 20+ days of fuel and food minimum
- **Hull Monitoring**: Repair when below 70% (costs 200cr per 10%)
- **Credit Banking**: Save 500+ credits for emergency shopping
- **Morale Management**: Keep morale above 30% to avoid chad desertion risk (at ≤10% morale)

### Waypoint Strategy
- **First 3 Waypoints** (Moon, Mars, Ceres): Skip unless critical - events still only 25-50%
- **Jupiter** (800M km): STOP here if low on supplies - last chance before speed/event increase
- **After Jupiter**: Skipping = 80M km/day BUT 75% event rate (very dangerous!)
- **Speed vs Safety**: Stopping reduces speed (1×) AND event rate (25% or 50% depending on base speed)
- **Emergency Stops**: Always stop if fuel or food below 15 days OR if you can't handle 75% event rate

### Event Handling
- **Watch Resource Changes**: Post-event screen shows exactly what changed (before → after)
- **Safe Choices**: 80-100% success rate, smaller rewards, good for tight resources
- **Risky Choices**: 50-70% success rate, larger rewards, only when you can afford losses
- **Chad Deaths**: Reduce food consumption (3 units/day per chad lost)
- **Learn Patterns**: Events repeat - learn which choices work best

### Speed Optimization
- **Jupiter Doubles Speed**: Base speed 20M → 40M km/day after 800M km mark
- **Gravitational Assist**: Skip waypoints for 2× multiplier (stacks with Jupiter boost!)
- **Maximum Speed**: 80M km/day (40M base × 2.0 multiplier) after Jupiter
- **Event Trade-off**: Higher speed = more events (25% → 50% → 75%)
- **Journey Times**:
  - All stops: ~140 days (25% event rate early, 50% after Jupiter)
  - Balanced: ~97 days (50% events after Jupiter)
  - Speed run: ~48 days (75% event rate, extremely risky!)

### Advanced Tips
- **Credits from Events**: Some events reward credits - hunt these opportunities
- **Chad Morale Quotes**: Low morale triggers different chad personality quotes
- **Hull in Detailed View**: Hull not shown in main display - check Status view
- **Map Planning**: Use "🗺️ Map" to see waypoint distances and plan stops
- **Return to Waypoint**: Use "↩️ Return to Last Waypoint" if you accidentally skip

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Bot Not Responding
- **Check**: Is the bot online and configured?
- **Solution**: Contact the administrator
- **Alternative**: Try sending `/start` again

#### Session Lost
- **Cause**: Session expired or server restart
- **Solution**: Start a new game with `/newgame`
- **Prevention**: Play regularly to avoid expiration

#### Commands Not Working
- **Check**: Are you using the correct command format?
- **Solution**: Try the full command (e.g., `/continue` instead of `/c`)
- **Alternative**: Use the button interface

#### Game Stuck
- **Check**: Do you have an active session?
- **Solution**: Use `/status` to check your current state
- **Reset**: Use `/abandon` to start fresh if needed

## 🚀 Advanced Features

### Waypoints & Strategic Decisions

**Seven Waypoints** between Earth and Uranus:
1. **Moon** (25M km) - First checkpoint
2. **Mars Base** (240M km) - Inner solar system
3. **Ceres Asteroid Station** (450M km) - Asteroid belt
4. **Jupiter Gas Mine** (800M km) - ⚡ **SPEED BOOST UNLOCKS HERE** ⚡
5. **Saturn Outpost** (1.4B km) - Outer solar system
6. **Deep Space 69** (2.1B km) - Deep space void
7. **Uranus Arrival** (2.8B km) - Destination!

### Stop or Skip Strategy

At each waypoint (except destination), you face a critical decision:

#### 🛒 Stop for Supplies
- Access shop (buy fuel, food, hull repair)
- Rest to restore hull (+20%) and morale (+30%)
- Meet local character with hilarious story
- **Trade-off**: Lose gravitational assist (speed → 1×)

#### ➡️ Skip Waypoint
- Continue at current speed
- Gain/maintain gravitational assist (2× speed multiplier)
- No shop access, no rest option
- **Trade-off**: Miss resupply opportunity

### Speed Mechanics (Critical!)

**Base Speed Progression:**
- **Days 1-40** (Earth to Jupiter): 20M km/day base
- **Days 41+** (After Jupiter): 40M km/day base (permanent boost!)

**Gravitational Assist:**
- **When Active**: 2× speed multiplier
- **How to Gain**: Skip a waypoint
- **How to Lose**: Stop at a waypoint
- **Stacking**: Works with Jupiter speed boost (40M × 2 = 80M km/day!)

**Journey Time Examples:**
- **Stop at all waypoints**: ~140 days (20M km/day average)
- **With Jupiter boost, stops as needed**: ~97 days (balanced approach)
- **Skip all waypoints**: ~48 days (speed run, very risky)

### Hunting System

**Hunt Resources** button appears while traveling (not at waypoints):
- **Purpose**: Find extra supplies between waypoints
- **Mechanic**: Board derelict ships and scavenge
- **Rewards**: Fuel, food, credits, or combinations
- **Events**: 8+ hunt-specific events (single-resource rewards)
- **Cost**: No direct cost, but events may have risks
- **Strategy**: Hunt regularly to supplement tight starting resources

### Map Navigation
- **Progress Tracking**: See how far you've traveled
- **Waypoint Notifications**: Get alerts at important locations
- **Distance Monitoring**: Track remaining distance to Uranus
- **Route Planning**: Plan your journey through the solar system

### Leaderboards
- **Speed Demon**: Fastest journey times (all victories)
- **Perfect Captain**: Flawless runs (5 chad + 100% hull)
- **Space Tycoon**: Most credits at arrival
- **Resource Master**: Best resource management (fuel × 1 + food × 0.5)
- **Event Master**: Best net resource gains from events

### Who's Playing
View active players and their journey progress:
- **Access**: `/frens` command or "Who's Playing" button
- **Shows**: All players with activity in last 7 days
- **Information**: Distance, days elapsed, chad count, last activity
- **Sorting**: By distance traveled (leaders at top)
- **Visibility**: See finished journeys (victories) and active travels
- **Community**: Connect with fellow travelers to Uranus

### Rest System

Available only when stopped at a waypoint:
- **Hull Restoration**: +20% hull integrity
- **Morale Boost**: +30% chad morale
- **Food Cost**: Uses food (chad × 3 units)
- **No Fuel Cost**: Resting doesn't consume fuel
- **Strategic Timing**: Use when hull <70% or morale <50%
- **Returns to Waypoint**: After resting, you stay at the waypoint view

### Aesthetic Themes

Choose your visual style from Launch View or Main Menu:
- **Default**: Classic space aesthetic with realistic visuals
- **Anime**: Japanese anime-inspired style
- **Pixar**: 3D animated Pixar-style visuals
- **Rick & Morty**: Rick and Morty animated universe
- **Pixel Art**: Retro 8-bit/16-bit pixel art style

**Note**: Aesthetics are cosmetic only - game mechanics remain identical
